Brew Crew

Privacy Policy — last updated July 29, 2026

Brew Crew never collects your name, email, contacts, location, photos, or any personal identity — and never tracks you across other apps or websites.

What stays on your device

  • All game progress (levels, coins, café layout, settings) is stored on your device.
  • If you're signed in to iCloud, your save also syncs through your own Apple iCloud account so it follows you between your devices. We run no account system and cannot see your save.

Anonymous gameplay statistics

To balance the game (level difficulty, economy tuning, crash rates), Brew Crew sends us anonymous gameplay events — things like "a shift was won", "a level took 30 moves", session lengths, and crash reports via Apple's MetricKit.

  • These events are tied only to a random install ID (a coin-flip identifier created on first launch) — never to your name, email, device serial, or advertising ID. We could not identify you from this data if we tried.
  • They go to our own server in the EU. There are no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs in the app — none.
  • Data is automatically deleted after 180 days.
  • You can turn this off anytime in Settings → Anonymous gameplay stats; switching it off also erases anything queued on your device.

Purchases

Coin packs are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details — we only record the same anonymous event as above (which pack, tied to the random install ID).

Music Corner

Tapping a playlist opens the Spotify app or website. Nothing about you is sent — it's an ordinary link. Spotify's own privacy policy applies inside Spotify.

Your choices

  • Opt out of gameplay statistics in Settings at any time.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all local data; iCloud saves can be removed in iOS Settings → Apple ID → iCloud.
  • Questions or a data request? Contact can@creamcoff.com — include your random install ID if you have it (we cannot look you up otherwise, by design).
