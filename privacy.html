This policy describes Brew Crew's game, optional gameplay statistics, diagnostic information and connected features. For privacy questions or requests, contact can@creamcoff.com.

Brew Crew on Google Play is published by ANALOG DIGITAL ART MEDYA LIMITED SIRKETI, which is responsible for processing personal data collected by the Android game and its first-party services. The privacy contact is can@creamcoff.com.

Gameplay statistics and diagnostics

Gameplay statistics are enabled by default. When enabled, the game sends events to our first-party analytics service at brewcrew.creamcoff.com to understand gameplay, improve the game and diagnose reliability problems. These events include:

  • An app-generated random installation identifier, event and session identifiers, event times, platform, app version and build.
  • Game interactions such as sessions, tutorial progress, shifts, levels, virtual currency activity and furniture viewing, purchases and placement. Some new events are available only in newer builds.
  • Diagnostic counts and performance summaries. iOS can report crash/hang counts and launch, responsiveness and memory summaries; Android reports crash counts and rendering-stall summaries, including delay duration and how often visual effects are reduced after a stall.

The random identifier lets us connect events from the same app installation. It is pseudonymous, not an advertising identifier or a claim that the data is anonymous. It may persist through a device backup or restore. Analytics events do not include your name, email, payment-card details, raw purchase tokens or raw crash stacks. We do not use these events for advertising across other companies' apps or sell them to data brokers.

In Settings, turn off Gameplay stats (called Anonymous gameplay stats in older builds) to stop collection and sending through this event pipeline and clear its pending on-device queue. An in-flight request may already have reached the server. Turning the setting off does not erase events already stored on the server. The game remains playable with this setting off.

Crash reports and a bounded gameplay diagnostic log are also recorded locally on the device. Local diagnostic recording continues when Gameplay stats is off; raw diagnostic files are not automatically uploaded by these components. If you choose to provide files to support, review their contents first.

Game saves and connected services

Your game progress, café arrangement, settings and saved playlist links are stored on your device. On iOS, supported saves that are not connected to a purchase account can synchronize through Apple's iCloud key-value service when your Apple account is available. A café connected to a purchase account uses a wallet and save bound to that device; it does not use this iCloud save service. Signing in does not provide cross-device restoration of that café or its remaining currency. The account wallet is excluded from device backups. Other app data may be retained or restored by operating-system backups where enabled.

The iOS game can submit scores through Game Center when that feature is available and authenticated. Apple's account and service settings govern these features. Android currently has a local game save; there is no Brew Crew iCloud or cross-device account-save service on Android.

The game fetches remote game configuration from our service even when Gameplay stats is off. These configuration requests do not include the analytics installation identifier in their request body or URL. Our service uses Hetzner hosting and Cloudflare network protection. These providers receive connection information, including IP addresses, to deliver and protect requests. Our server access logs record IP addresses, request times, requested paths, response status and size, and user-agent and referrer information when supplied. These logs are configured to rotate daily and retain 14 archived files; periodic cleanup can take additional time. This connection processing continues when Gameplay stats is off. We do not use this information to infer your location for gameplay analytics.

Music links open Spotify only when you choose them. Spotify handles that activity under its own policies. Reminders use the device's notification system and its permission settings.

Purchases

Where purchases are offered in a released build, Apple or Google processes payment through its store. The game receives product and transaction information to deliver virtual items and recover unfinished transactions. Payment-card details are handled by the store, not Brew Crew. Virtual currency and item activity can appear in gameplay statistics when enabled.

Where account-backed iOS purchases are available, Sign in with Apple connects purchases to a Brew Crew account. Our service processes Apple's app-specific user identifier, a generated Brew Crew account identifier, session identifiers, a random device-wallet identifier, and product, transaction, store-environment and delivery records. We use these to authenticate access, verify purchases, deliver virtual items once and resume unfinished delivery. Account and purchase processing operates independently of optional Gameplay stats. The analytics installation identifier is not purchase authentication.

We process Apple's identity token and one-time authorization code transiently to verify sign-in. We do not request your name or email as account-profile fields; Apple's identity token may contain claims such as an email address. Short-lived challenge records and token hashes help prevent replay. Where Apple account lifecycle support is enabled, we store Apple's refresh and access tokens encrypted so we can revoke access when you delete the account. The app keeps its Brew Crew session credentials in device-only secure storage.

Deleting a purchase account

Where purchase accounts are available, open Settings → Purchase account → Delete purchase account in the app. Confirm the deletion and authenticate again with the same Apple account. The app shows a pending state while the request or local cleanup is unfinished and lets you check its status. Once deletion is confirmed, associated account, session, provider-token and purchase-delivery records are removed from our active service, and the café, progress, coins and jade on this device are cleared, including unused purchased currency. You must explicitly choose to start a new café. Signing out of purchases does not delete the account or café. Account deletion does not automatically request an Apple refund.

We attempt to revoke the Apple sign-in tokens. If this cannot be completed, account-data deletion proceeds and the app explains how to disconnect Brew Crew manually in your Apple account settings. A minimal completion-status record, without the deleted account identifier or provider tokens, is scheduled for removal 24 hours after completion; periodic cleanup can take additional time. The device retains protected recovery information while deletion is pending and completion markers to resume cleanup and prevent an old save from returning.

This action does not remotely erase offline devices, Apple's purchase records, Game Center data, historical iCloud saves, operating-system backups or independent gameplay analytics. The app blocks adoption of the old iCloud café after deletion. Use the separate analytics request process below for gameplay events.

Storage, access and deletion

Gameplay events are stored in our PostgreSQL analytics service. Access to individual events and exports is restricted. Raw events are scheduled for deletion after they are older than 180 days; periodic cleanup can take additional time. Historical backup copies may retain data longer than the live analytics retention period and are not automatically removed by this cleanup. You can include backup copies in a deletion request. Before a backup is restored into active use, completed privacy deletions must be reapplied.

You can request access or deletion by emailing can@creamcoff.com. Include the analytics installation identifier shown in Settings if you want us to locate your gameplay events. Do not send a password, payment-card details or purchase token. We may need further information to verify a request. Uninstalling the app does not itself delete server events, iCloud data or operating-system backups.

If you email support, we use the information you choose to send to respond and investigate your request. Do not include sensitive information that is unnecessary for the issue.

Changes

We update this page when the game's data handling changes. Store disclosures must match the actual released builds and connected services. This page does not mean that new account or purchase features have been activated.
