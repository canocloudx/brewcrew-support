Brew Crew

Privacy Policy — last updated 2026

Brew Crew does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Specifically:

  • The app makes no network requests of any kind.
  • There is no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDK of any kind.
  • There is no account creation, login, or user profile.
  • All game progress (levels, coins, café upgrades, settings) is stored locally on your device only, and is deleted if you uninstall the app.
  • No data is shared with any third party, because no data ever leaves your device.

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future update adds optional cloud save), this page will be updated first and the in-app privacy label will reflect it.

Questions? Contact can@creamcoff.com.
